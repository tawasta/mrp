======================================
Quality Control OCA: Inspection E-mail
======================================

* Adds a "Send by E-mail" button to inspections, available once the
  inspection has been completed 
* Opens the mail composer wizard pre-filled with a
  dedicated e-mail template, the inspection's PDF report attached,
  and the recipient defaulted to the inspection's Partner
* Adds an "Inspection E-mail Sent" field
  automatically set when an e-mail is sent through the composer, with a
  "Not Sent by E-mail" filter to find inspections not yet
  communicated.

Configuration
=============
* The e-mail template ("Quality Control: Inspection Results") can be
  edited from Settings -> Technical -> Email Templates.

Usage
=====
* Once an inspection is done, click "Send by E-mail" on the
  inspection form.
* Review/adjust the recipients and message, then send.
